Mission

Why this
exists.

Attention isn't broken. It's just pointed somewhere else.

01
Meet people where they scroll

Short attention spans aren't a flaw — they're an adaptation. Patholytica works inside the feed environment and delivers real knowledge there.

02
Surface your blind spots

The algorithm watches how you read and quietly fills the gaps — without you having to diagnose them yourself.

03
Short-form done right retains

Four minutes of focused vertical reading can outperform forty minutes of distracted long-form. Format isn't the problem. Mismatch is.

04
Your feed, your rhythm

Every person has a pace and density that holds them. Patholytica finds yours. You stay in flow instead of bouncing out of it.
