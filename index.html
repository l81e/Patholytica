Nephrology
Oliguria → AKI until proven otherwise
Urine output below 0.5 mL/kg/hr for 6+ hours is the clinical trigger. Classify immediately: pre-renal (volume), intrinsic, or post-renal obstruction. Your workup branches from there.
💡 Keyword trigger: oliguria in ICU patient → check Foley first, then volume status

ESRD dialysis access → fistula first
AVF (arteriovenous fistula) is gold standard: lowest infection rate, best longevity. AVG if no suitable vein. Tunneled catheter = last resort for permanent access.
