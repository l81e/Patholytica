How it works
Reads every article's tags, then for each unique tag creates or updates a library named algotag(name), adding only articles not already linked — no duplicates. Membership is checked at scan time, so tags that are already fully up to date land directly under "Already Synced" — you don't need to click Sync just to find out nothing was missing.
